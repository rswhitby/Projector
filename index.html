The direction of the projectors

ARCH 5511 · parallel projection: orthographic and oblique

Object

A curved surface has no edges on it. What you see as its outline is a silhouette, and the silhouette moves when the projectors turn — it belongs to the drawing, not to the object.

Projection

Glass box

Three orthographic projections, each with projectors normal to its own plane. Unfolding the box is the sheet layout. Planes sit between the eye and the object: third angle.

Direction of view

Projectors stay parallel and stay normal to the plane. Only the plane turns. Azimuth 0, elevation 0 recovers a single orthographic view.

Plane held true

Projectors are parallel but no longer normal to the plane. One face stays true at full scale; the depth ratio is a convention, not a measurement. Circles on the true face stay circles.

Station point

Projectors converge on a point. Push the station point away and the pencil of projectors flattens toward parallel.
